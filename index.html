The Art of Du'a
WHAT IS DU'A · 1 OF 3
Du'a Is Worship Itself
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Du'a is worship." Then he recited: "And your Lord said: Call upon Me, I will respond to you. Indeed, those who are too proud to worship Me will enter Hell humiliated."

Du'a is not a last resort. It is the highest act of worship — a direct line to Allah, available to every believer, at every moment, in every language.
Abu Dawud (1479) — Sahih · Qur'an 40:60
WHAT IS DU'A · 2 OF 3
The Most Generous
The Prophet ﷺ said: "There is nothing more noble in the sight of Allah than du'a."

Allah is Al-Karīm — the Most Generous. He is not burdened by your asking. He does not grow tired of your requests. He does not think less of you for coming back again and again.

The one who does not ask Allah angers Him. The one who asks Him pleases Him.
Ibn Majah (3829) — Sahih · Ahmad (9701)
WHAT IS DU'A · 3 OF 3
Du'a Changes Qadr
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Nothing increases lifespan except righteousness, and nothing repels the decree except du'a."

Du'a is not passive acceptance — it is the means by which Allah has decreed that things change. Making du'a is itself part of the qadr. The person who makes du'a and the person who does not are in entirely different positions before Allah.
Ibn Majah (90) — Hasan · al-Tirmidhi (2139)
۞
ETIQUETTE · 1 OF 4
How to Begin
Start with Bismillah and Alhamdulillah. Then send salawat on the Prophet ﷺ. The Prophet ﷺ heard a man making du'a without praising Allah first — he said: "This man rushed." He then said: "When one of you prays, let him begin by glorifying his Lord and praising Him, then send salawat on the Prophet ﷺ, then ask for whatever he wishes."

Open every du'a session with: Alhamdulillah... Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad... then ask.
Abu Dawud (1481) — Sahih · al-Tirmidhi (3477)
ETIQUETTE · 2 OF 4
Face the Qiblah, Raise Your Hands
Face the qiblah when making du'a — the Prophet ﷺ turned toward it on important occasions such as Badr and Arafah.

Raise both hands to shoulder height, palms facing upward. The Prophet ﷺ said Allah is too shy to let a servant raise his hands to Him and return them empty.

After du'a, wipe your face with both palms. This is the Sunnah.
Abu Dawud (1485, 1488) — Sahih · al-Tirmidhi (3556)
ETIQUETTE · 3 OF 4
Certainty, Repetition and Insistence
The Prophet ﷺ said: "When one of you makes du'a, let him be firm in asking and not say: O Allah, give me this if You will — for there is no one who can compel Allah."

Ask with certainty that Allah hears you and will respond. Repeat your du'a three times. The Prophet ﷺ used to repeat his du'a three times. Do not give up. Persistence in du'a is itself beloved to Allah.
Sahih al-Bukhari (6338) · Sahih Muslim (2679)
ETIQUETTE · 4 OF 4
Ask for Yourself First
The Prophet ﷺ said that when Jibril made du'a, he began with himself first before the Prophet ﷺ. Scholars say: begin your du'a with yourself, then include your parents, your spouse, your family, and the Muslim Ummah.

Be specific. Ask for real things — your marriage, your provision, your health, your akhirah. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Ask Allah for everything — even the strap of your sandal." No du'a is too small.
al-Tirmidhi (3385) · Sahih · Abu Dawud
۞
WHAT WEAKENS DU'A · 1 OF 2
Haram Provision
The Prophet ﷺ mentioned a traveller: dishevelled, dusty, raising his hands to the sky saying "O Lord, O Lord" — yet his food is haram, his drink is haram, his clothing is haram — "so how can his du'a be answered?"

This does not mean haram income makes du'a void — it means it is a veil between you and acceptance. Pursue halal provision earnestly. And if you have haram in your life, increase istighfar — it lifts the veil.
Sahih Muslim (1015)
WHAT WEAKENS DU'A · 2 OF 2
Impatience and Abandonment
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The du'a of a servant will continue to be answered as long as he does not ask for something sinful or the cutting of family ties, and as long as he is not hasty." They asked: what is being hasty? He said: "He says: I made du'a and I made du'a and I have not seen a response — so he becomes frustrated and abandons du'a."

This is the most common way du'a is lost. Keep going.
Sahih al-Bukhari (6340) · Sahih Muslim (2735)
۞
BEST TIMES · 1 OF 5
The Last Third of the Night
Allah descends to the lowest heaven in the final third of every night and calls out: "Who is calling upon Me that I may answer? Who is asking of Me that I may give? Who is seeking My forgiveness that I may forgive?"

This is the single greatest window for du'a in every 24 hours. It is available every night — not just in Ramadan, not just on Laylatul Qadr. Wake before Fajr. Even 10 minutes. Ask.
Sahih al-Bukhari (1145) · Sahih Muslim (758)
BEST TIMES · 2 OF 5
In Sujood
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The closest a servant is to his Lord is when he is in sujood — so make plenty of du'a."

After saying Subhana Rabbiyal A'la three times, you are in the closest position a human body can take before Allah. This is when you whisper. In Arabic, in English, in whatever words you have. Pour out everything. No position is better.
Sahih Muslim (482)
BEST TIMES · 3 OF 5
Between the Adhan and Iqamah
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Du'a between the adhan and iqamah is not rejected."

This is a short window — sometimes just a few minutes. But it is guaranteed. Make it a habit: when you hear the adhan, respond to it word by word, then immediately make du'a before the iqamah comes. Five times a day, you have a guaranteed window.
Abu Dawud (521) · al-Tirmidhi (212) — Sahih
BEST TIMES · 4 OF 5
After Every Obligatory Salah
The Prophet ﷺ was asked which du'a is most readily heard. He said: "In the last part of the night, and after the obligatory prayers."

Do not rush away after salah. After your tasbih (33/33/34), after Ayatul Kursi — stay seated and make du'a. This is why the After Salah section in this app exists. The moment after salah is sacred.
al-Tirmidhi (3499) — Hasan
BEST TIMES · 5 OF 5
The Friday Hour
On every Friday there is a blessed hour in which no Muslim asks Allah for anything except that it is granted. The most supported opinion is that this window falls in the last hour before Maghrib.

Protect this hour every Friday. Make du'a continuously. The Sa'at al-Istijabah timer in this app was built for this.
Sahih al-Bukhari (935) · Sahih Muslim (852)
۞
HOW ALLAH ANSWERS · 1 OF 3
Three Ways Du'a Is Answered
The Prophet ﷺ said: "There is no Muslim who makes du'a to Allah with a du'a that contains no sin and no cutting of family ties, except that Allah gives him one of three things: either his du'a is answered promptly, or it is stored up for him in the Hereafter, or an equivalent evil is diverted from him."

They said: then we will ask for more! He said: "Allah gives even more."

No du'a is ever wasted. Every word reaches Allah.
Ahmad (11133) — Sahih · al-Hakim
HOW ALLAH ANSWERS · 2 OF 3
Delayed Is Not Denied
Ibrahim (AS) made du'a for a son for decades before Ismail (AS) was born. Zakariyya (AS) made du'a for a child his entire life before Yahya (AS) was given to him when both he and his wife were very old.

Allah's timing is not your timing. When something you asked for is delayed, it is not a sign that Allah did not hear. It is a sign that He is preparing something — either the thing you asked for at the right time, or something better, or a reward so great it will make the wait seem nothing.
Qur'an 21:89–90 · Qur'an 37:100–101
HOW ALLAH ANSWERS · 3 OF 3
Barriers to Answer
Some things can delay or veil the response:

• Haram provision or earnings
• Sins not repented from — increase istighfar
• Asking for something sinful
• Cutting family ties
• Impatience and abandoning du'a
• A heart that is heedless and distracted while asking

None of these make du'a permanently void. Repentance clears every barrier. The door is always open.
Sahih Muslim (1015) · al-Tirmidhi (3479)
۞
DU'A AND QADR · 1 OF 2
Du'a Is Part of the Decree
Some ask: if everything is already decreed, why make du'a? The answer is that du'a itself is part of the decree. Allah decreed that certain things will happen — and He decreed that they will happen through du'a.

It is like asking: if my rizq is decreed, why work? Because working is the means by which the rizq comes. Du'a is the means by which what Allah wills for you comes to you. The one who does not ask cuts himself off from the means.
Ibn al-Qayyim, al-Da' wal-Dawa' · Ibn Majah (90)
DU'A AND QADR · 2 OF 2
The Shield and the Arrow
Ibn al-Qayyim said: "Du'a and bala' (calamity) are engaged in a struggle. Du'a prevents bala' from descending, or lifts it after it has descended, or lightens it."

He compared du'a to a shield and calamity to an arrow. A strong shield stops the arrow. A weak shield may slow it. An arrow that has already struck can still be extracted by du'a. This is why the believer never stops making du'a — even when calamity has already arrived.
Ibn al-Qayyim, al-Da' wal-Dawa'
۞
SPECIAL TYPES · 1 OF 3
Du'a of the Oppressed
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Fear the du'a of the oppressed — for there is no barrier between it and Allah."

The one who has been wronged, the one who has no worldly power to seek justice — their du'a goes directly to Allah with no veil. This is one of the most powerful forms of du'a in the Sunnah.

Never oppress anyone. And if you have been oppressed, know that your du'a carries extraordinary weight before Allah.
Sahih al-Bukhari (1496) · Sahih Muslim (19)
SPECIAL TYPES · 2 OF 3
Du'a for Your Brother in His Absence
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The du'a of a Muslim for his brother in his absence is answered. At his head is an appointed angel — whenever he makes du'a for his brother with good, the angel says: Ameen, and may you have the same."

When you make du'a for someone who does not know you made it for them — an angel is saying the same du'a back for you. Make du'a for your parents, your spouse, your family, your friends, the Ummah.
Sahih Muslim (2733)
SPECIAL TYPES · 3 OF 3
The Du'a of a Parent for Their Child
The Prophet ﷺ said that three du'as are answered without doubt: the du'a of a father for his child, the du'a of the oppressed, and the du'a of the traveller.

Your parents' du'a for you is one of the most powerful forces in your life — seen and unseen. Honour your parents so their hearts make du'a for you in those private moments. And make du'a for them, as Ibrahim (AS) did: Rabbighfir lī wa liwālidayya.
Abu Dawud (1536) — Sahih · Qur'an 14:41
۞
FIVE CONDITIONS · 1 OF 2
The Five Conditions of Accepted Du'a
Ibn al-Qayyim summarised the conditions of an accepted du'a:

1. Certainty — Certain that Allah hears and will respond
2. Sincerity — Asking only Allah, not showing off
3. Presence of heart — Focused, not distracted
4. Facing the qiblah with raised hands — Physical posture of humility
5. Persistence — Not abandoning du'a if delayed

Work on one condition at a time.
Ibn al-Qayyim, Zad al-Ma'ad
FIVE CONDITIONS · 2 OF 2
Never Stop Asking
Allah says: "Call upon Me, I will respond to you."

This is not a maybe. This is not conditional on your level of iman or the number of your sins. It is a promise from Al-Karīm — the Most Generous — to every single believer who calls on Him.

Your du'a list is not just an app. It is your record of conversation with Allah. Every du'a you have ever made has been heard. Every one is either answered here or waiting for you on the Day you meet Him. Keep asking.
Qur'an 40:60 · Ibn Kathir, Tafsir
